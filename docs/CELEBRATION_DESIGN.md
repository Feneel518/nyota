# Additional celebration scenes

The editor now offers Carnival, Pool Party and Grah Shanti in English and Gujarati. Each has its own animated scene and matching static invitation motif. Existing invitations also recognize these names automatically; Grah Santak, Grah Shantak, Griha Shanti and Gruh Shanti map to Grah Shanti. Owners can keep their own ceremony wording and override the animation.

## Research and art direction

- **Grah Shanti / ગ્રહ શાંતિ**: a Gujarati pre-wedding prayer ceremony for blessings and harmony. The scene uses a havan, kalash and lamps. Family practices vary; the illustration does not prescribe a ritual sequence. Reference: [Gujarati wedding ceremony guide](https://asianwedding.photography/gujarati-wedding-ceremony-guide) and [a couple's description of their Gruh Shanti](https://www.zola.com/wedding/poojaandsamson/faq).
- **Carnival / કાર્નિવલ**: a festive fair with interactive stalls, games and colourful decoration. The scene uses striped stalls, bunting and a slowly turning wheel. Reference: [wedding carnival hosting and concepts](https://mytribe.in/anchortallani/wedding-event-hosting/wedding-carnivals-unique-concepts).
- **Pool Party / પૂલ પાર્ટી**: a relaxed poolside gathering. The scene uses turquoise water, parasols, gentle ripples and a floating ring. Reference: [Avani's Indian wedding celebration guide](https://www.avanihotels.com/uploads/minor/avani/documents/vhhv/wedding/avani-_-huahin-_-indian-_-weddings.pdf).

All scenes retain the selected couple and outfits. Animation follows the existing pause, offscreen and reduced-motion controls. No external artwork or additional animation dependency is used.
